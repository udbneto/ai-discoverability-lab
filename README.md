# AI Discoverability Lab

Applied research initiative exploring how AI systems retrieve, interpret, synthesize, and recommend business information across conversational interfaces and modern LLM ecosystems.

---

## Thesis

AI systems are becoming a new discovery layer.

Increasingly, users are not:
- searching websites directly,
- browsing traditional search engines,
- or navigating digital channels manually.

Instead, they are interacting with:
- conversational assistants,
- retrieval-augmented systems,
- AI-generated recommendations,
- and synthesized responses produced by large language models.

This creates a new challenge:

> How can organizations understand, measure, and improve their visibility inside AI-mediated discovery systems?

This repository exists to explore that question.

---

## Mission

The AI Discoverability Lab focuses on:
- conversational visibility,
- retrieval observability,
- semantic positioning,
- telemetry generation,
- benchmark datasets,
- and AI-mediated discoverability experiments.

The project operates primarily as:
- an experimentation lab,
- observability initiative,
- and retrieval intelligence research platform.

---

## Why This Matters

Traditional SEO was designed for:
- indexed pages,
- ranking systems,
- and explicit search results.

AI-mediated discovery changes the interaction model entirely.

Modern LLM ecosystems:
- synthesize information,
- compress sources,
- infer relevance probabilistically,
- and may recommend entities without exposing traditional ranking mechanics.

This creates important unanswered questions:

- What makes an entity discoverable by AI systems?
- How stable are AI-generated recommendations?
- How should conversational visibility be measured?
- Can retrieval patterns be observed longitudinally?
- Which signals influence AI-mediated discoverability?
- How should organizations interpret probabilistic visibility?

## Core Principles

### Evidence over speculation
The project prioritizes:
- experiments,
- telemetry,
- benchmarks,
- and reproducible findings.

### Learning velocity over feature velocity
The goal is not to build a large platform quickly.

The goal is to:
- reduce uncertainty,
- generate evidence,
- and improve understanding of AI-mediated discoverability.

### Lightweight experimentation
The repository intentionally avoids:
- premature SaaS complexity,
- enterprise architecture,
- unnecessary infrastructure,
- and over-engineering.

### Transparency
Findings, limitations, assumptions, and uncertainties should be documented whenever possible.
